# Finding gear

Find gear still in the water on a north-up chart map or in the Gear list. The Map tab shows gear in the water, my position and the nearest gear's distance and direction. Days, day markers, the Map filter and the collected layer are in [Days and the Map filter](days-and-map-filter.md).

## Sub-features

- Map tab (the default tab): NOAA chart tiles by default from the fixed `default028mm` grid. A `Base map` radio `SegmentedControl` chooses `Chart` or `Street` (OpenFreeMap); the chosen item shows an underline, takes one Tab stop and switches by arrow keys. Rotation and pitch are off.
- The Map tab, a past trip's detail and every other map use the shared `GearMap`, which draws gear, groups and the Gear here chooser over `ChartMap`.
- Gear in the water shows as buttons with the kind icon and number, and a time-in-the-water label underneath. Each button's name reads the gear name, distance and point, time in the water, and rough position when applicable.
- Gear whose drawn markers would touch shows as one group button: the lead gear's chip, its day only when every gear in the group went in that day, and a `+N` segment bordered like the layer of the other gear (in the water first), with one `!` when any of it is past gear left uncollected. The group button's name summarizes the group: the visible text, `here` or `in this area`, the days when they differ, the count in each layer when the layers mix, and how many are uncollected, for example "Pot 1 +1 more here; 1 of 2 uncollected". It does not list the individual gear. Gear at one spot never splits at any zoom. Tapping the group opens the Gear here chooser, titled `{n} gear here` or `{n} gear in this area`, which lists every gear by its own name, label, day and status; each opens that gear's sheet. A group draws no time label, so it takes no room below its chip. Every drop the filter shows is drawn exactly once: as its own marker, or as a member of a group of two or more. No marker is empty, and there is no group of one. A chain of drops spaced so that one's time label reaches the next, but a group chip would not, still groups. `e2e/chart-map.test.ts` proves this for six drops in a line at 393 × 852 (both themes), 402 × 681 and 320 × 640, and `src/lib/features/map/groups.test.ts` proves the partition across zooms, orders and featured leads.
- While the map shows all gear, it shifts so drawn gear clears the map controls, a map notice and the credits where the map has room, and shifts again when a notice appears or leaves. While Undo shows, the newest drop's marker and time label also clear the toast, measured from the real credits height. If the newest drop is in a group that can't be shown clear of them, the map zooms in just enough to draw it on its own. At 320 × 640 with a map notice showing this still holds. When the camera can't move (after I pan, zoom or Show on map), a notice that appears draws beneath gear, time labels and my position, and moves no gear or control; `e2e/chart-map.test.ts` owns that proof.
- The Undo toast covers the reserved dock row below the map, so it covers no gear, credits or map control, and a toast or status strip never resizes the map view. `e2e/chart-map.test.ts` owns that proof at 402 × 681, 320 × 640 and 320 × 568.
- My position: a dot inside a ring wide enough to show above and below a gear chip at the same place, with an accuracy area. It is named `Current position` for a ready fix, with "rough, within about …" when worse than 50 m and "heading …" while moving at 0.5 m/s or more when the fix reports a heading, and `Last position` for a stale fix. A past trip's map shows no position marker.
- Nearest-gear card in the dock: a button named "Nearest gear: <name>" that shows `Nearest`, the distance and direction; see [Compass](compass.md) for its arrow.
- Buttons, so dragging is never needed: Chart map and Street map, Zoom in, Zoom out (unavailable at the limits), Show all gear (`Fit shown gear` while the filter hides some gear), and Centre on my position, which is disabled while there is no current fix.
- Tapping gear on the map opens its gear sheet without Show on map. Show on map appears in a sheet opened from the Gear list, switches to the Map tab and focuses that gear. It also rings that gear's marker, or the group holding it, in magenta and marks it `aria-current="true"`. The ring is separate from focus: it stays when focus moves on and when the gear's sheet opens and closes, and the zoom buttons keep it. It clears when you move the map yourself (drag, pinch, wheel or arrow keys), when you press Show all gear, or when the map closes. It is never saved.
- Map notices: `Loading map` while the Street style loads. When base tiles or the Street style fail to load, or the Street style has not fully arrived after 10 seconds, the notice names no cause unless the browser reports it is offline: `Map can't load` while online, `Map offline` while offline. When the map service answers base tiles busy or failing (HTTP 429 or 5xx), or refuses or sends an unusable Street style: `Map service not answering`. `Retry map` shows while online for `Map can't load` and `Map service not answering`. Missing tiles (404) show no notice. Gear and position still render either way, and the notice clears once a map tile arrives.
- Map credits: a chip opens a scrollable `Popover` titled `Map credits` for screen readers, with the NOAA chart link and `Not for navigation`, or the OpenFreeMap, OpenMapTiles and OpenStreetMap links. On a short viewport it scrolls inside the screen and shows each link whole as Tab reaches it. Escape closes it and returns focus to the chip. A tap outside, for example on Filter or Drop pot, does what it says and closes the credits.
- Chart zoom is capped at XYZ z16, which is NOAA service level 14. The map overscales beyond that, up to z19.

## How to get to it (user POV)

Open Krab. The Map tab is the first tab. Turn on location and drop a pot or net; the gear appears on the map at your position. Tap the gear to open its sheet. Choose the Gear tab at the top, tap a row, then tap Show on map to return to the map at that gear.

## Driving it with Playwright

Run `bun scripts/verify-krab/run.ts` through the parent skill's bounded lifecycle. [finding-gear.ts](../../../../scripts/verify-krab/finding-gear.ts) owns the exact actions, assertions and evidence filenames.

Covers finding a drop on Map and from Gear, opening its sheet, Show on map focus and `aria-current`, Street switching and recentering. The separate chart-alignment journey reads land/water pixels from real NOAA tiles at public landmarks at two zooms, retaining sampled tiles and alignment evidence. These landmarks are never a browser position or drop.

## Gotchas

- MapLibre's unlayered `.maplibregl-map { position: relative }` beats Tailwind's layered utilities. Keep the map container `size-full` inside a separately positioned wrapper, or it collapses to 0 px, which hides the chart and makes gear untappable even though Playwright reports it visible.
- The map takes the height its caller gives it and sets no minimum of its own. A minimum taller than the Map tab's slot on a 320 × 640 screen pushed the map under the panel below, clipping the credits and misplacing the Undo clearance.
- The Map tab is the default, so the map is mounted before any fix exists. The map notice (`Map can't load`, `Map offline` or `Map service not answering`) is a second status region beside the toast; check toasts with `toastOf(page)` from `e2e/fixtures.ts` (the toast item inside the `Messages` region) rather than any `role="status"`.
- MapLibre's canvas is a region named "Map view"; the tab's region is "Map". Use `exact: true`.
- Rapid Zoom in presses interrupt each other's animation and never reach the limit. Press once and wait for the button to become unavailable before pressing again.
- Both journeys use the real network: NOAA and OpenFreeMap must be reachable from the container. Mid-ocean NOAA tiles are blank (no coverage), so the chart looks like plain sea at `MADE_UP_SEA`.
- Page JavaScript cannot read `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`. A successful `getImageData` on a `mode: 'cors'` fetch is the WebGL-relevant CORS proof.
- Course and rough labels, the zoom cap, privacy, the map notices and the credits Popover are owned by `e2e/chart-map.test.ts` with routed tiles. This recipe gathers evidence and does not replace that proof.
